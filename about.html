how I got here

I started with HTML when I was ten. I don’t remember exactly why — probably because I wanted to make a website for something I cared about and the only way to do that was to learn how. CSS came next, then JavaScript, then a long stretch of building small things that didn’t work very well but taught me what working things were supposed to feel like.

At a summer camp, I learned from a real Software Engineer more about how real websites are designed. Once I got home I began to learn about a bunch of new things. One of these new things was react. From there I created BitWise.

Now I’m still learning and diving deeper into Software Engineering. I am learning new technologies like Redis, Springboot, AWS, and C.
